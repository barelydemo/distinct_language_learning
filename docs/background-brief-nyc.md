# Lexical — background brief (6 New York scenes)

You are painting six background scenes for **Lexical**, an anime visual novel. This part is set in **New York City, late spring 1998**. Characters are drawn as separate sprites and placed on top of these backgrounds. **Paint the empty place: no people in the foreground.** The bottom of each image will sit behind the dialogue box and the characters' legs.

## 2. Scenes

### 2.1 `east_village_day`: St. Marks Place, East Village (bright late-spring morning)
Brownstones with zig-zag fire escapes; a record shop and a vintage clothing store with hand-painted signs; a blanket of VHS tapes for sale on the sidewalk (no seller); skateboards leaning on a stoop; a sticker-plastered payphone; lampposts thick with paper flyers. Down the block, a chrome diner with a cardboard **HELP WANTED** sign in the window. Keep the near-centre pavement clear.

### 2.2 `russo_room`: Mr. Russo's apartment behind a Bowery hostel (warm afternoon)
A cramped old apartment, warm light through lace curtains: a Formica kitchen table with a cigar box on it, a rotary telephone, a stovetop espresso pot on a hot plate, a record player with a stack of LPs, and a whole wall of black-and-white family photographs in mismatched frames — one showing two young men laughing on brownstone steps. Nostalgic, quiet, a little dusty. Keep the space in front of the table open.

### 2.3 `coffee_shop`: Café Orbit near NYU (rainy afternoon)
A narrow 1990s indie coffee shop: mismatched thrift-store sofas and armchairs, a hand-lettered chalkboard menu, exposed brick covered in poetry-night and band flyers, a chunky beige Macintosh by the register, a hissing espresso machine, steamed-up front windows with rain streaking down, warm amber lamplight. Cosy and bohemian.

### 2.4 `street_fair`: Avenue A street fair (June, dusk)
Avenue A closed to traffic: striped food tents with sausage-and-pepper grills and trays of powdered zeppole, a band's empty flatbed-truck stage with instruments, an open fire hydrant spraying water, string lights between fire escapes, a pink-and-orange sky above tenement rooftops. Crowd only as soft, faceless shapes far down the avenue. Keep a clear patch of street in the near centre.

### 2.5 `broadway_theatre`: lobby of the Calloway Theatre, Broadway (intermission, evening)
The gilded lobby of a 1920s Broadway theatre: red velvet ropes and carpet, a sweeping marble staircase, crystal chandeliers, gold-leaf mouldings, framed posters of invented classic musicals (no real titles), a small bar with plastic cups, and through the glass doors the neon billboards of late-1990s Times Square (invented, no real brands). Grand and glowing.

### 2.6 `precinct`: front room of an NYPD precinct house (morning)
A tall scuffed wooden front desk with a brass rail, walls painted institutional green, a cork board of wanted posters and notices, a clanking radiator, beige computers and a desk phone, a row of plastic chairs, a coffee pot, morning light through tall barred windows. Worn, busy, a little tired. Keep the space in front of the desk open.
