# Lexical — background brief (4 times of day)

Some scenes are visited both at night and in the morning or daytime, but each has only a night picture. Each of these is the **same place as an existing background at a different time of day**: same room, same street, same camera, same furniture, with only the light, the sky and the life in it changed.

## 1. Technical spec

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | JPG, high quality, **1280 × 720** (16:9) or larger 16:9 |
| Reference | Attach the existing background named below for each one, and **match its composition, camera angle, perspective and every fixed object exactly**. Change only the time of day |
| Style | The same painterly anime-background style as the reference |
| People | None in the foreground or middle ground. At most a few soft, faceless figures far in the background |
| Text | Painted signs as in the reference; no captions, watermarks or borders |
| Composition | Keep the centre third clear at standing height and the bottom 25% simple, as in the reference |

## 2. Scenes

### 2.1 `hostel_room_morning` (reference: `web/public/scenes/hostel_room.jpg`)
The same six-bed Hostel Sakura dorm at **early morning, about 7 a.m.** Soft pale-gold sunlight through the window and lace of the curtains, long gentle shadows across the floor. Through the window, Tokyo in morning haze: **Tokyo Tower unlit**, pale red-and-white against a light blue sky. Paper lanterns off. Bunk curtains drawn on the other beds; one rumpled blanket. Quiet, fresh, hopeful.

### 2.2 `ueno_street_day` (reference: `web/public/scenes/ueno_street_night.jpg`)
The same Ueno street under the elevated railway at **late morning, sunny**. Neon signs switched off and plain in daylight, lanterns unlit, shop shutters up, delivery crates on the pavement, sunlight striping down between the tracks overhead. Dry pavement (no night reflections). A few soft, faceless commuters far down the street. Busy-but-ordinary weekday Tokyo.

### 2.3 `ueno_park_dawn` (reference: `web/public/scenes/ueno_park_night.jpg`)
The same lamp-lit path, bench and pond in Ueno Park at **dawn, about 5:30 a.m.** The lamps are just switching off; a cool lavender-to-peach sky; thin mist on the pond; dew on the bench; cherry trees catching the first light. Cold, still, beautiful: someone has just woken up on that bench.

### 2.4 `hostel_room_nyc_morning` (reference: `web/public/scenes/hostel_room_nyc.jpg`)
The same Bowery Bunkhouse dorm at **early morning, about 7 a.m.** Weak grey-gold light through the window, the radiator quiet, other bunks curtained. Through the window, Manhattan rooftops and water towers in morning light, with the **Twin Towers pale in the haze** (it's 1998). Quiet and hopeful.
